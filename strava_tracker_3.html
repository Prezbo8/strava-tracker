Quick setup (2 min):
1. Go to strava.com/settings/api
2. Set Authorization Callback Domain to prezbo8.github.io
3. Set Website to https://prezbo8.github.io
4. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret
5. Enter them above and click "Connect with Strava"
6. After authorizing, you'll be redirected back here automatically
